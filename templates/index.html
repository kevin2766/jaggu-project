Problem Statement #1

The Deepfake Threat in India

India is the world's largest consumer of mobile data and the biggest user base for WhatsApp and YouTube. However, this vast connectivity brings severe threats:

  • Viral Misinformation: Unchecked deepfake videos inflame communal tensions, spread fabricated political statements during elections, and impersonate public figures.
  • Financial & Impersonation Scams: AI voice cloning and face swapping power "digital arrest" scams and fake video calls that dupe elderly citizens out of their life savings.
  • Lack of Citizen Tools: Ordinary citizens and local journalists have zero accessible, trustworthy tools to check videos in their regional languages before forwarding them.
Our Solution

SatyaDrishti (सत्य दृष्टि) AI Platform

SatyaDrishti is a lightweight, multi-signal deepfake detection platform tailored specifically for Indian media ecosystems:

  • Zero-Barrier Access: Web-based, responsive, works directly on smartphones and desktops without requiring specialized GPU hardware.
  • 3 Clear Decision States: "Possible manipulation detected", "No strong signs detected", and critically, "Unable to assess" for degraded footage.
  • Plain-Language Multi-Lingual Support: Instant explanations in 7 Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, English).
  • Citizen Action Guidance: Direct integration with the National Cybercrime Portal (`cybercrime.gov.in`) and the 1930 Helpline.
Technical Architecture

Multi-Modal Forensic Pipeline

Rather than relying on a single black-box model that fails when video is compressed, SatyaDrishti uses a four-pillar forensic pipeline:

  • 1. Spatial Boundary Forensics: Detects Laplacian edge gradient disparities and blending seams where a swapped face is composited onto a host body.
  • 2. 2D FFT Spectral Analysis: Exposes the periodic high-frequency grid artifacts left behind by GAN decoders and diffusion upsamplers.
  • 3. Biological Dynamics: Tracks natural human eye-blink rates (12-20/min) and flags staring avatars or synthetic eyelid flutter.
  • 4. Chrominance & Motion Stability: Evaluates YCrCb skin-tone shifts between face mask and neck contours, alongside inter-frame warp jitter.
Ethical Compliance

Responsible AI & The "Unable to Assess" State

A key hackathon guideline is: "Do not label a real person as a liar or claim a video is definitely fake based only on an AI score."

  • Avoiding False Accusations: Low-resolution (under 360p) or blurry WhatsApp forwards are explicitly categorized as "Unable to assess" rather than generating a reckless false positive.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Clarity: We provide forensic clues (e.g. "Abnormal Eyelid Flutter at 2.4s") rather than an opaque pass/fail, enabling fact-checkers to inspect the exact keyframes.
  • Clear Warning Banners: Prominent reminders that automated AI results are advisory warning signs, not legal verdicts.
Impact & Reach

Empowering 900+ Million Connected Citizens

How SatyaDrishti protects Indian society and democracy:

  • Electoral Integrity: Election Commission observers and journalists can verify controversial political speeches within seconds.
  • Stopping WhatsApp Forward Cascades: Equips WhatsApp group administrators and community leaders with an easy verification link before a clip goes viral.
  • Law Enforcement Integration: Provides police cyber cells with printable ISO/IEC 27037 forensic dossiers featuring cryptographic SHA-256 hashes.
Limitations & Roadmap

Honest Limitations & Next Steps

Current Limitations (Hackathon Honest Disclosure):

  • Heavily compressed 144p videos (5th generation WhatsApp forwards) often lack sufficient facial pixels for micro-expression analysis.
  • State-of-the-art Sora-grade whole-frame diffusion generators do not leave boundary seams, requiring audio phoneme alignment and deep temporal transformers.

Future Roadmap:

  • WhatsApp Bot integration: Send a video to an official WhatsApp number to receive an instant SatyaDrishti verdict card.
  • C2PA Cryptographic Content Credentials verification for camera-native provenance.
